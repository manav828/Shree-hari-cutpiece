A Loom with a Legacy

Each Solstice Throw is crafted by a collective of fifth-generation weavers in the central highlands. Using traditional back-strap looms, the process for a single throw takes nearly three weeks from the raw cotton spinning to the final knotted fringe.

We partner directly with these artisans, ensuring equitable pay that is 3x the local living wage, directly supporting community infrastructure and education.
