Curiosity Corner

Do you ship internationally? expand_more

Yes, we ship to over 50 countries. Shipping rates and delivery times vary by location and will be calculated at checkout.

How do I care for my handmade ceramics? expand_more

Our ceramics are generally dishwasher safe, but we recommend hand-washing with mild soap to preserve the unique glazed finishes for years to come.

Are your products truly limited batch? expand_more

Absolutely. Because our artisans work by hand and use seasonal natural dyes, we only produce small quantities (usually 50-100 units) of each design per quarter.
