The Razorpay account belongs to you (the client). You create your own Razorpay merchant account, complete KYC, and share API keys with us for integration. How We Work

Our simple 3-phase delivery process from start to launch.

Phase 1 — Day 1–2
Discovery & Setup
What happens:
• We discuss your business, target audience, and goals
• Collect brand assets (logo, colors, product photos)
• You choose your plan & theme
• You create your Razorpay merchant account

You provide: Logo, brand colors, product list with photos & prices, Razorpay API keys

Build & Review

Phase 3 — Day 10–15
Launch & Training
What happens:

• Store goes live on your domain
• Admin panel training (video call / recorded)

• Remaining 50% payment collected
• Support period begins

Terms & Conditions

Important terms that govern our working relationship.

💳 Payment Terms

• One-time plans: 50% advance before work begins, 50% before going live
• Monthly plans: Setup fee in full + first month's payment before work begins
• Revenue sharing: Setup fee (if any) before work begins. Revenue share due by 7th of each month
• Accepted: UPI, Bank Transfer, Cash
• All prices are inclusive — no hidden charges. No GST applicable.

🔄 Revisions & Changes

• Up to 2 rounds of revisions included in all plans during build phase
• Major scope changes (new features, redesign) after approval will be quoted separately
• Content changes (text, images) during support period: unlimited
• Theme change after launch: charged as per add-on pricing

🛡️ Hosting & Maintenance

• Free hosting period as per plan (6 or 12 months)
• After free period, monthly hosting & maintenance fee applies
• Maintenance includes: server uptime, security patches, basic bug fixes, database backups
• If hosting fee is not paid for 2 consecutive months, website may be taken offline

📋 Ownership & Data

• Website code is proprietary and remains with Shree Hari E-Commerce at all times. Source code is not shared, transferred, or provided to the client under any plan.
• Client's data: Client owns all their content, product data, customer data, and order data at all times. Data export is available on request.
• Razorpay account and all payment/settlement data belongs entirely to the client.
• Domain: Domain purchased by/for the client belongs to the client.
• We build, host, manage, and maintain the store — the client focuses on running their business.

❌ Cancellation & Refund

• Before work starts: Full refund (minus 10% processing fee)
• During development: Advance is non-refundable, but work done till date will be delivered
• Monthly plans: Cancel after minimum period with 30 days notice
• Revenue share: Exit via buyout fee as per contract

⏰ Support Terms

• Support hours: Monday – Saturday, 10 AM – 7 PM IST
• Response time: as per plan (24hr / 48hr / same day)
• Support covers: Bug fixes, minor text/image changes, guidance
• Support does NOT cover: New feature development, marketing, content creation, order management
