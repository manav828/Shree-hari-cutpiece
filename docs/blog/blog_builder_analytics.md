# Blog Analytics Guide

## Overview
This guide explains the blog analytics dashboard, how metrics are computed, and how to interpret results.

## Dashboard Sections
- Summary cards for views, published posts, and product clicks.
- Views-over-time trend for the selected range.
- Referrer and device breakdowns.
- Conversion event totals (product, collection, CTA, share).
- Top posts by CTR.
- Content health flags for low traffic, SEO completeness, and related products.

## Metric Notes
- Views are derived from page_view events.
- CTR uses product_clicks divided by views.
- Health flags are calculated from published posts only.

## Operational Tips
- Use the 7/30/90 day selector to see short-term vs long-term shifts.
- Investigate low-traffic posts for SEO or content updates.
- Fill missing SEO fields to clear the SEO Incomplete flag.
